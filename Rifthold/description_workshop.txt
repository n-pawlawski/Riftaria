[b]Rifthold[/b] replaces the open Terraria world with a persistent hub and a run-based loop.

You live in [b]the Hold[/b], a small sealed base. [b]Rift Sigils[/b] open unstable pocket worlds that are generated fresh every time you enter. Fight to the [b]Rift Warden[/b] at the far end, kill it, and carry the loot and experience home. Deeper rifts drop the sigils for the tier above them.

[b]Blocks cannot be mined.[/b] The layout in front of you is the level, and gear comes from rifts rather than from ore.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*] A persistent hub that grows as you clear deeper rifts
[*] Procedurally generated rift maps, discarded after every run
[*] Experience, levels, and allocatable stat points
[*] Tiered progression gated by character level
[/list]

[b]Requires Subworld Library.[/b]
